GOOD TO KNOW
- The SVG files were traced from the 793 px PNG, so edges are smooth but not an original drawing. If the original vector (AI, EPS or PDF) from whoever designed the logo exists, use that for large prints and signs.
- The 1586 px and 3172 px PNGs are enlarged from the original, so they are bigger but not sharper. Use the SVG for very large sizes.
- Suggested minimum size: about 120 px wide on screen, about 25 mm wide in print.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of a lowercase letter, and do not stretch, recolour or rotate it.
- The colour logo has a dark outline, so it works best on white, light grey or other light backgrounds. Use the white version on dark backgrounds.
- Printing: screen colours shift a little in CMYK. Ask the printer for a proof.

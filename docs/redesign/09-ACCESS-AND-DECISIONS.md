# 09 — Account-manager checklist

Most discovery should be performed through the authorised agency/admin access, not sent back to the client as a technical questionnaire. Record answers privately when they contain account or legal/private details. Do not publish passwords, payment information, customer data or account exports here.

## Obtain/confirm through existing access

1. Accepted IONOS agency permission and the right project; ability to reach WordPress admin, SFTP/SSH, private backups/database and staging or manual-clone facilities. Exact hosting package and supported runtime.
2. Theme/builder/plugin versions, update entitlements and licence ownership; active checkout/gateway/shipping/tax/mail/cache/SEO/consent settings. Identify which party pays renewals.
3. Published/hidden pages, product/category inventory, redirects, menus, account endpoints, forms, scheduled jobs and any existing marketing/TikTok integration. Capture baseline functionality.
4. Search Console/analytics/Merchant Center access if already used, plus newsletter provider/list and verified social/stream link. Access is only needed for the parts actually being delivered.

## Ask the merchant only for the decisions/facts we cannot inspect

| Question | Why it changes the build | Default pending answer |
|---|---|---|
| Which current items are delivered sealed versus opened live, and what does the live buyer receive? | Correct labels, service expectations and order notes | Preserve current workflow; no new assumptions or merged SKUs. |
| How should the shipping-upgrade purchase relate to a live order, and which reference is required? | Prevent orphan postage purchases/double charges | Preserve existing direct SKU/notes; exclude from discovery rows. |
| Are the OP14 names genuinely different products, and what is the correct category for the Pokémon item currently under One Piece? | Prevent wrong fulfilment and bad filters | Keep IDs/data until confirmed; flag ambiguities. |
| Are current stock, contents, shrink/condition and image rights accurate? | Truthful cards/product pages and usable hero assets | Existing availability stays; no unsupported factory/shrink claims. |
| Confirm dispatch window, shipping areas/fees, trading details and approved policy wording | Operational claims, checkout and trust | Published wording is evidence for review, not newly certified legal text. |
| Who receives contact messages and manages newsletter/stock alerts? | Real form handling and service ownership | Reuse current working path; optional alerts remain disabled. |
| Does the owner want a calmer cream/ink storefront with brand accents? | Single visual direction for the surprise presentation | Present this recommendation on real desktop/mobile screens, not a long style questionnaire. |

## Extra-scope decisions

No automatic new spending. New paid search/stock-alert/newsletter/CMP services need current quotes, renewal owner and approval. New TikTok bidirectional stock/order sync, loyalty/rewards, paid recurring subscriptions, grading, blog/SEO landing pages or platform migration are separately scoped. Keep the redesign progressing while those decisions remain optional.

## Before go-live

Confirm exact release window, approver, test payment/refund arrangement, selective change manifest and rollback owner. Client sign-off should review working staging screens and the preserved purchase journey. Theme activation must never overwrite a live order database from an older staging copy.

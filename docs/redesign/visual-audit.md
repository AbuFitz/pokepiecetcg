# Rendered desktop research notes

6 November 2026. Read-only external browser research completed for the five supplied shops. These notes summarise that report and are qualitative observations, not measurements. No mobile-width inspection or downloadable screenshots were returned. No sign-in, purchase, customer-form submission or intentional basket mutation was requested.

| Store | Rendered composition observed | Decision for PokePiece |
|---|---|---|
| PokePieceTCG | Dark cosmic background with orange/yellow detail; large centred symbol/tagline; one featured shipping-upgrade card; search overlay and right cart sidebar. WoodMart-specific classes reported. | Product visibility and merchandising need a fundamental rethink; reuse proven native capability after inventory. |
| Anti Rip Rip Club | White product-first presentation; red/black identity, prominent sold-out states; no traditional hero observed. | Preserve directness while giving PokePiece one purposeful branded opening. |
| Reevy | Dark grey branding, purple/blue accents; large seasonal/preorder banner; newsletter prompt observed. | Retain useful category structure, avoid a viewport dominated by an offer or immediate signup interruption. |
| Coddys Collection | Blue identity and Japanese landscape illustration; hamburger navigation; products absent from the inspected first view. | Learn from a coherent identity, but keep desktop navigation and actual stock more accessible. |
| OnThePull | Purple/gold illustrated world, dense game navigation, newest-arrival carousel; account/signup overlay reported. | Borrow structured discovery; avoid excess visual effects and interrupting login prompts. |

Reported exact font names were not verified, so none are asserted here. Popup visibility is session/time-dependent; “not observed” does not mean absent. Theme family identification from classes is strong evidence, not a substitute for installed-theme/version/licence inventory. The proposed calmer layout is a project design judgment; these references do not demonstrate measured conversion outcomes.
